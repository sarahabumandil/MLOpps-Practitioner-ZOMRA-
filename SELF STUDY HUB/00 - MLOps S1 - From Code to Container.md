## 🧠 Why this matters (context from Aya)
- 87% of ML models never reach production (Gartner 2024).
- MLOps skills carry a ~28% salary premium (Burtch Works 2024).
- If you're a DevOps engineer moving into MLOps, you already know ~60% of this.
- The tools taught here (FastAPI, MLflow, Docker, etc.) are just **examples** — the *concepts* are what matter. Tools change; concepts don't.
- This course maps to Level 0/1 → Level 2 of the [[01 - MLOps Maturity Model|maturity model]].
